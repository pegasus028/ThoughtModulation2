# Modulation Desk — picture pack

The app already shows its own built-in illustrations. To use your own pictures instead, upload them to the **img/** folder of the GitHub repo with exactly these names (GitHub Pages is case-sensitive; .png, .PNG, .jpg or .webp all work). Any picture you don't upload keeps the built-in drawing.

**Style line to add to every prompt** (matches your existing scene slides): photorealistic, cinematic lighting, 16:9, no text overlay, the student is a Thai female Royal Thai Air Force finance officer in navy-blue uniform, all characters fictional.
